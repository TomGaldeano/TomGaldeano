🧪 How to Play

  • Click / tap a bottle to select it as the pouring bottle (it will lift up).
  • Click / tap another bottle to pour the liquid into it.
  • You can only pour if the target bottle has space and its top color matches the pouring color (or the target bottle is completely empty).
  • All consecutive matching liquid layers will pour automatically into the available space.
  • Win the game by filling each bottle with 4 segments of the same color!
